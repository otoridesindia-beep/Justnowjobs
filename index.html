YOUR DAILY PRACTICE

Interview question for the day

What a strong answer can include

01

Research the company

Understand their product, data stack, and recent news. Prepare one thoughtful question about how their team uses data.

02

Brush up on SQL and fundamentals

Practise joins, window functions, aggregations, and core statistics. Talk through your reasoning out loud as you solve.

03

Tell stories with your projects

For each project, explain the problem, your approach, the tools you chose, and the measurable result.

04

Use the STAR method

Answer behavioural questions with Situation, Task, Action, and Result. Keep each answer to about two minutes.

05

Explain it simply

Practise describing a technical idea to a non-technical person. Clear communication is a top skill for data roles.

06

Ask smart questions and follow up

Ask about team goals, data quality, and success in the first 90 days. Send a short thank-you note within 24 hours.
